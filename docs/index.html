  • Renewal plans lag condition in a few asset classes. In the 2026 disclosures the largest gaps between assets already in poor condition and the five-year replacement forecast are in Centralised plant (25.2% in H1/H2, 13.6% planned); LV OH/UG Streetlight circuit (10.8% in H1/H2, 1.2% planned); Distribution OH Open Wire Conductor (11.5% in H1/H2, 3.9% planned).
  • Renewal spend has met forecast overall. Across all networks, actual asset replacement and renewal capex was 106% of forecast in DPP3 (2021-2025), up from 101% in DPP2 (2016-2020); 1 of 29 networks spent less than 80% of forecast.
  • Planned outage minutes have grown. Median planned SAIDI rose from 43 to 138 minutes between 2013 and 2025, in line with more work being done on the networks, while unplanned SAIDI from defective equipment stayed between 24 and 39 minutes.
  • Big national movements often come from one network re-grading. 194 one-year moves of 20 points or more were found. For example, WEL Networks SCADA and communications equipment operating as a single system went from 0% to 48% in 2026, moving the all-network figure by +9.8 points; Powerco 33kV Switch (Pole Mounted) went from 28% to 1% in 2023, moving the all-network figure by -6.7 points; Aurora Energy Centralised plant went from 71% to 0% in 2022, moving the all-network figure by -5.5 points. These are flagged for checking rather than read as real deterioration or improvement.
  • Data checks. 13 network asset class records were left out because their condition grades do not add up to 100%.

Method and limits

Condition grades are assessed by each network and practice differs between networks; Schedule 12a also carries a data accuracy score from 1 to 4, which is kept in the outputs. Disclosures before 2019 used a different four-grade scale and are not included. Planned replacement is a forecast, not a commitment, and assets in H2 may reasonably be scheduled beyond five years. Schedule 7 compares actual spend with the forecast made in an earlier plan, so a variance reflects both delivery and changes of plan. Distribution networks are not Transpower's grid, but they report under the same Commerce Commission regime, which makes them the most complete public data set for this kind of portfolio analysis.
